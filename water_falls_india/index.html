Dudhsagar Falls

Located on the Mandovi River, this "Sea of Milk" is one of India's tallest falls.

Jog Falls

The second-highest plunge waterfall in India, located in Sagara, Karnataka.

Nohkalikai Falls

The tallest plunge waterfall in India, located near Cherrapunji, Meghalaya.
