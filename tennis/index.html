Fundamental Rules

  • Scoring: Points follow 0, 15, 30, 40, and Game.
  • Deuce: When both players reach 40-40, two consecutive points are needed to win.
  • In/Out: If the ball hits any part of the line, it is considered "in".
  • Service: The server must stand behind the baseline and serve diagonally.
  • Sets: Usually, men play best of 5 sets in Slams, while women play best of 3.
  • Let: If a serve hits the net but lands in the box, it is replayed.
